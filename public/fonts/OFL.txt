Kept in the repo rather than fetched from Google at render time, because
opengraph-image.tsx needs the actual font bytes and a share card that silently
loses its typeface when a third-party request fails is worse than 80KB here.
Space Mono is already the app's face for every price and time (DESIGN.md →
Type), so the card and the site agree.

It lives under public/ rather than beside the route because Turbopack cannot
fetch a file: URL — `fetch(new URL("./x.ttf", import.meta.url))`, which is the
pattern the Next docs suggest, fails in dev with "not implemented... yet...".
Serving it over HTTP is one mechanism that works in both dev and production,
and it's the same one the card already uses for the mascot.
